Quick Information

Electronics are part of the BTU Lab and provides tools and materials building circuits.

About Electronics

The BTU Lab Electronic station gives students a place to work on projects involving wires and circuitry.

Students can use different tools to build their circuits.

Equipment

The Electronic has different tools available for circuitry projects.

  • Soldering Irons and the Soldering station
  • Power inputs and outputs
  • Prototyping materials
  • Other tools

Orientation

Safety

Always follow the BTU Lab's safety rules when using the Electronic station.

Do not use equipment that you have not been trained to use.

Need Help?

Ask BTU Lab staff if you have questions about a tool or are unsure how to use equipment safely.
