The Tools and the Talent...

General tools, equipment, and materials are labeled green. Power tools, mostly used in the wood shop, are more like... greeeeen!

The BTU Lab keeps lots of general tools on hand for all your inventing needs. The main space can accommodate dozens of people at once, and the lab is working on increasing project storage space.

Materials

The BTU Lab is not a free supply store. There are many ways to barter, including displaying your work in the lab, tagging the lab on social media, offering workshops, and volunteering to organize supplies.

If you need something, the lab asks that you give something in return.
