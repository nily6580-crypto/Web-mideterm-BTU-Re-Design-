About the BTU Lab

The ATLAS BTU Lab is an inclusive community makerspace. It supports projects involving art, design, engineering, and architecture.

The lab is open to CU Boulder students and provides access and training for tools used in 3D printing, woodworking, laser cutting, electronics, sewing, textiles, and more.

The lab's motto is: we make science fiction science fact, and vice versa.

The Lab Wears Three Hats

  • A student hackerspace
  • A residency for ATLAS graduate student desks
  • At times, a classroom

Getting Started

Who?

Anyone can gain access to the BTU Lab. The goal is to provide an inclusive environment for makers of all types.

How?

New members attend a 20-minute general orientation covering community expectations, safety, and equipment.

When?

Orientations, workshops, and other events can be found on the BTU Calendar.

BTU Lab Director

Zack Weaver

Director, BTU Lab and Assistant Teaching Professor, ATLAS Institute.

Zack helped establish the BTU Lab as an ATLAS PhD student in 2014. He later worked for an electronics company he co-founded and became co-director of BLDG 61 at the Boulder Public Library.

Zack believes makerspaces can play an important role in learning and community building.
