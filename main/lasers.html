The BTU Lab Laser Cutter gives students a place to work on projects involving acrylics, woods and more.

Orientation

Students may need to complete an orientation before using the Laser cutter.

Orientation helps students learn how to use the equipment correctly and safely.

Safety

Always follow the BTU Lab's safety rules when using the Laser Cutters.

Do not use equipment that you have not been trained to use.

Need Help?

Ask BTU Lab staff if you have questions about a tool or are unsure how to use equipment safely.
