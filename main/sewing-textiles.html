Quick Information

The Sewing & Textiles area gives students equipment and workspace for fabric and textile projects.

About Sewing & Textiles

The BTU Lab supports projects involving sewing, fabrics, textiles, and other materials.

Students can use the space for class projects, prototypes, and other creative projects.

Equipment

The Sewing & Textiles area has tools and equipment for different types of textile projects.

  • Sewing machines
  • Scissors
  • Measuring tools
  • Cutting tools
  • Needles
  • Thread
  • Other sewing tools

Materials

Various fabrics and other materials may be available in the lab.

The BTU Lab is not a free supply store. Students should ask lab staff before using materials.

Getting Started

Students should learn how to properly use the sewing and textile equipment before beginning a project.

Ask BTU Lab staff if you need help using a machine or choosing the correct tools for your project.

Need Help?

BTU Lab staff can help answer questions about sewing equipment, materials, and projects.
