Quick Information

Sewing and texiles are part of the BTU Lab as well as tools and materials for sewing.

About Sewing and Texiles

The BTU Lab sewing station gives students a place to work on projects involving fabric and other materials.

Students can use different hand tools and sewing machines to cut, sew and iron materials.

Equipment

The sewing station has different tools available for sewing projects.

  • Sewing machines
  • 4-thread Serger
  • Cutting Mats
  • Fabric Scissors
  • Knives
  • Pins & Needles
  • Iron & and Ironing Board
  • Fabrics

Safety

Always follow the BTU Lab's safety rules when using the sewing machines and irons.

Do not use equipment that you have not been trained to use.

Need Help?

Ask BTU Lab staff if you have questions about a tool or are unsure how to use equipment safely.
